What happens after I submit?

A producer will review your enquiry and reply by email with a price, turnaround time and any questions.

Do I pay anything to enquire?

No payment is needed to enquire. Work starts once you have approved the quote and the 30% payment is confirmed.

I only have photos or a rough idea. Is that enough?

Yes. Share your photos, sketches or a description of your idea, along with what you need to create. We can clarify the details when scoping the project.

I don’t know my budget. Can I still enquire?

Yes. Choose “Not sure yet” in the budget field and describe what you need so the team can scope the work.

Select the NDA option in the contact step to request an agreement before sharing confidential files.
