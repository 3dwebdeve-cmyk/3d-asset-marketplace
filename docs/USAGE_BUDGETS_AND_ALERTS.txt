EXTERNAL USAGE BUDGETS AND ALERTS
=================================

Rate limits protect the application from bursts. They do not replace provider-side
spending controls. Before public launch, configure provider budgets and alerts in
the account that owns each service.

REQUIRED GUARDRAILS
-------------------

- Stripe: alert at 50%, 80%, and 100% of the monthly processing-fee forecast,
  and on a failed-payment spike. Investigate unusual checkout volume.
- Brevo/email: alert at 50%, 80%, and 100% of daily sending quota. Check abuse,
  bounces, and reset/verification traffic.
- Cloudflare R2: alert at 50%, 80%, and 100% of monthly storage and egress
  budget. Review download volume, signed URLs, and large files.
- Sentry: alert at 50%, 80%, and 100% of monthly event quota. Fix noisy errors
  or lower tracing sampling.

APPLICATION CONTROLS
--------------------

- Checkout creation and confirmation: 10 requests/minute and 30/day per verified user.
- Admin uploads: 12 uploads/minute per administrator, alongside normal admin writes.
- Product images above 20 megapixels and avatars above 4 megapixels are rejected
  before storage.

RELEASE CHECKLIST
-----------------

1. Assign a named owner and notification channel for every provider alert.
2. Configure provider hard caps where available; an alert is not a cap.
3. Run `python manage.py check_security_smoke` and `python manage.py check_waf_readiness`
   after deployment.
4. Review Sentry webhook-failure and repeated-429 alerts for the first 24 hours.
